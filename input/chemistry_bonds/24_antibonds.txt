24. ANTIBONDS (Molecular Orbital Theory)
    When atomic orbitals combine destructively, creating a "node" 
    (zero electron density) between the nuclei. They push atoms apart.
    
      Sigma Antibond (σ*):
         (+)      (-)
        [Atom]|||[Atom]   <-- ||| = Node (repulsive force)
         (-)      (+)

      Pi Antibond (π*):
         (+)      (-)
          ~~  |||  ~~
        [Atom]|||[Atom]   <-- Destructive sideways overlap
          ~~  |||  ~~
         (-)      (+)
