16. 3D MOLECULAR GEOMETRY (VSEPR Theory)
    
    Using Wedges (coming OUT of page) and Dashes (going INTO page):
    
          H
          |
      H - C - H      (H, C, and top H are in the plane of the page)
         / \
       ◀H   H▶       (Left H is coming OUT, Right H is going IN)
      (Wedge)(Dash)
      
    Represents a Tetrahedral geometry (like Methane, CH₄).
