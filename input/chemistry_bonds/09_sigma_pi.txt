9. SIGMA (σ) AND PI (π) BONDS (Orbital Overlap)
   
   Sigma (σ): Head-on overlap (Stronger, allows rotation)
      (s)       (p)
       _         _
      / \       / \
     |   |=====|   |  <-- electron density directly between nuclei
      \_/       \_/
       *         *    <-- atomic nuclei

   Pi (π): Sideways overlap (Weaker, restricts rotation)
      (p)       (p)
       _         _
      / \       / \
     |   |     |   |  
      \_/       \_/
       *         *    <-- nuclei
       ||       ||
       ~~       ~~    <-- electron density above and below axis
