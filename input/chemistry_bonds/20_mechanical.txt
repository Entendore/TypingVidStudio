20. MECHANICAL BONDS (Supramolecular Topology)
    Not electron sharing, but physical interlocking of molecules!
    
    CATENANE (Interlocking Rings):
       _       _
     /   \   /   \
    |  A  | |  B  |      Rings cannot be separated without
     \ _ /   \ _ /       breaking a covalent bond.

       Stopper                 Stopper
