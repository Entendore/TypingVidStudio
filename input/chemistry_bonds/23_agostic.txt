23. AGOSTIC INTERACTIONS (Organometallics)
    A C-H bond acts as a ligand, donating electron density to an 
    electron-deficient transition metal (M).
    
                L
                |
          L ——— M ····· H ——— C — R
                |      (Agostic interaction)
                L
                
    The M·····H-C interaction stabilizes the metal center without 
    fully breaking the C-H bond.
