17. THREE-CENTER TWO-ELECTRON (3c-2e) BONDS (e.g., Diborane B₂H₆)
    Also known as "bridge bonds". Two electrons hold THREE atoms together.
    
                 H(bridge)
                /         \
       H(term)             H(term)
              \           /
               B         B     <-- No direct B-B bond!
              /           \
       H(term)             H(term)
                \         /
                 H(bridge)
                 
    The top and bottom H atoms each share their 1 electron 
    with BOTH Boron atoms simultaneously.
