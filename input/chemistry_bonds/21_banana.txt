21. BANANA BONDS (Bent Bonds in Strained Rings)
    Found in Cyclopropane (C₃H₃). Severe 60° ring strain prevents 
    normal head-on orbital overlap.
    
          C
         / \
        /   \       <-- Normal bonds would be straight lines
       C-----C
       
      ACTUAL ORBITAL OVERLAP:
          C
        _/ \_
      /       \     <-- Electron density bows OUTWARD
     C---------C       outside the internuclear axis, 
                       resembling a banana shape.
